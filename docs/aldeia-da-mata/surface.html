Two places to examine

Aldeia da Mata village

A 600 m patch around the village reference coordinate. Ground classification reveals the terrain between buildings. Areas beneath buildings and vegetation can contain interpolated ground.

Anta do Tapadão

A 400 m patch around its approximate published location, about 1.75 km from the village reference. The marker locates the known monument. Ground classification can remove standing stonework, so this view is suited to examining its surrounding terrain.

Location references from Aldeia da Mata, Anta da Aldeia da Mata and Anta do Tapadão. The two monument articles give nearby coordinates. Its marker uses the German article’s coordinate and is approximate.

How this surface was made

Detailed ground returns were averaged into 0.5 m cells. Empty cells use their nearest measured neighbour, followed by smoothing with a 0.5 m Gaussian sigma. Cells more than 5 m from a measurement remain blank. The solid mesh uses 2 m vertices, with shading calculated from the finer 0.5 m grid.

These are locally derived terrain models. The 0.5 m cell size describes the output grid. Local detail also depends on the spacing and classification of the underlying measurements. Height exaggeration changes the display.
