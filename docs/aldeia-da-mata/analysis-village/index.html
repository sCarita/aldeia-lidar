Check the support.
Dark cells are missing data. Amber support cells are interpolated. Buildings and classification gaps can leave shapes that resemble terrain features. The 0.5 m grid does not establish 0.5 m survey accuracy.
How to review

Inspect the same geometry under several lighting directions. Record its shape and plausible modern or natural causes. Check local relief and support, then inspect original ground returns before interpreting a candidate. Existing roads, buildings, walls and drainage are useful explanations to test.

This set contains no archaeological labels. Dates, function and novelty require independent evidence. Overlapping images are not independent samples.
